Who dies if E pushes the stone?

The puzzle, rebuilt as a rigid-body physics simulation. Press Run and let Box2D settle the argument, then change the settings and see whether the answer holds.

The verdict

Press Run and E gives the boulder a shove.

    Physics

    Changing a setting resets the scene. The URL keeps your settings, so you can share a scenario.

    How it's modelled

    • The geometry is traced from the puzzle drawing at 55 px = 1 m. People are the size they are drawn (A 2.1 m, B 1.8 m, D 1.36 m, so the top of D's head pokes 13 cm out of the hole) and weigh 70 kg. Both stones are 1.6 m across.
    • E's boulder defaults to ten times the mass of the seesaw stone. A seesaw passes on only part of the boulder's energy, so with similar masses the stone never clears the pillar.
    • E pushes the boulder horizontally with the chosen force until it rolls out of arm's reach (0.6 m).
    • Someone dies if a stone changes their velocity by more than 3 m/s within 1/60 s (a hard hit, or being crushed), if anything else does so by more than 10 m/s (a bad fall), or if they touch a spike.
    • Everything is a rigid body, people included, so D's head is a solid bump in the boulder's path. The seesaw is a plank on a pin joint; its teeth and the floor spikes are solid. Physics runs at a fixed 240 Hz, so the same settings always give the same result.
    • Built with Planck.js, a JavaScript port of Box2D.
